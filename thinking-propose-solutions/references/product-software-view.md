# Product/Software Decision View

Use alongside the shared workflow in `SKILL.md` for proposals about a product
or software system, including the product/software aspects of a mixed decision.

## Additional criteria

Consider agreed user behavior, workflows, business rules, data meaning, and
compatibility.

## Required disclosure

Distinguish technical means that preserve agreed behavior from product
decisions that define or change it. Label the latter **Product decision
required** and explain the user-visible or business consequence, even when the
interface looks unchanged. An internal refactor is not automatically a product
decision.
