# General Decision View

Use alongside the shared workflow in `SKILL.md` for decisions outside a product
or software system, or for the general aspects of a mixed decision.

## Additional criteria

Consider agreed outcomes, policies, responsibilities, commitments, and effects
on affected people.

## Required disclosure

Identify choices that establish or change these expectations as policy,
organizational, personal, or other context-appropriate decisions. Explain who
is affected and whose decision is needed. Do not impose product terminology.
